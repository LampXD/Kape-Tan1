What is Kape-Tan About?

Kape-Tan is a small coffee brand that provides student with high-quality, affordable coffee options for studnets. As well as aesthetic appeal, Kape-Tan focuses serving great coffee in the go for students that are always on the move or in need of quick caffeine fixes.

Kape-History!

It all started with an espresso maker, the love for coffee, and a peaked interest for sharing his love for coffee. Buboy tan wanted to see how far this espresso maker can go. After a while he got the idea that his love for coffee could be shared with other people, he turned his passion into a business and started kape-tan!
